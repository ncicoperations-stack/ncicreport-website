NATIONAL CYBER INTELLIGENCE CENTER

About the National Cyber Intelligence Center

The National Cyber Intelligence Center (NCIC) supports cybercrime victims through structured reporting, evidence organization, intelligence assessment, and public awareness. Our goal is to help individuals prepare clear, organized, and comprehensive reports that support investigative efforts while promoting a safer digital environment for everyone.

OUR MISSION

Empowering Victims. Strengthening Cyber Intelligence.

To empower cybercrime victims through structured reporting, evidence organization, intelligence-driven assessment, and public education while supporting safer digital communities and more effective investigative outcomes.

WHO WE ARE

Supporting Better Cybercrime Reporting

The National Cyber Intelligence Center (NCIC) is a public cybercrime reporting and intelligence support platform dedicated to helping individuals prepare complete, well-organized, and evidence-based cybercrime reports.

We work alongside established reporting processes by helping victims organize digital evidence, verify available information, and build a clear chronological account of what happened. A structured report provides investigators with clearer information, making it easier to understand the incident and determine the appropriate course of action.

Beyond reporting, NCIC promotes public cyber awareness through educational resources, scam prevention guidance, intelligence insights, and practical information that helps individuals recognize, prevent, and respond to cybercrime.

OUR MISSION

Helping Victims. Supporting Investigations. Educating the Public.

NCIC is committed to improving the quality of cybercrime reporting by helping citizens prepare complete, organized, and evidence-based reports while promoting cyber awareness and supporting intelligence-led investigations.

Support Victims

Help victims prepare complete reports by collecting relevant information, preserving digital evidence, and documenting incidents accurately.

Organize Evidence

Verify available information and organize evidence into a clear chronological storyline that presents events in an understandable manner.

Strengthen Intelligence

Structure information so recurring patterns, criminal methods, and emerging cyber threats can be identified more effectively.

Educate the Public

Publish educational resources, scam alerts, prevention guidance, and recovery information that help individuals stay safe online.

WHAT WE DO

Supporting Every Stage of the Reporting Process

NCIC provides structured guidance that helps transform individual cybercrime reports into organized case files that are easier to understand, assess, and present to the appropriate authorities.

Structured Reporting

Our reporting system guides victims through a comprehensive process that captures the essential details investigators typically require.

Evidence Collection

We encourage victims to gather screenshots, emails, messages, financial records, transaction receipts, and other digital evidence before important information is lost.

Verification & Timeline

Information is reviewed for completeness and organized into a logical chronological timeline, helping present a clearer picture of the incident.

Intelligence Support

Well-structured reports contribute to broader intelligence analysis by identifying recurring scam techniques, criminal behaviour, and emerging cyber threats.

OUR VALUES

Principles That Guide Everything We Do

Integrity

HOW WE HELP

Reporting & Intelligence Workflow

Every report follows a structured workflow designed to improve clarity, preserve evidence, and create a complete case file that supports intelligence assessment and appropriate investigative review.

1

Report Submission

Victims submit incident details through the secure NCIC reporting system with supporting documents and available evidence.

2

Evidence Organization

Information is reviewed, supporting material is organized, and important evidence is grouped into a structured case record.

3

Verification & Timeline

Available information is verified where possible and arranged into a chronological sequence that clearly explains how the incident occurred.

4

Intelligence Assessment

Reports are assessed to identify recurring scam techniques, investigative indicators, and potential links with broader cybercrime activity.

5

Prepared for Authorities

A well-organized report helps citizens present their information more clearly to the appropriate authorities, supporting faster review and more informed action where applicable.
