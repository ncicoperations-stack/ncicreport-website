CYBER AWARENESS

Knowledge Is Your First Line of Defense

Cybercrime continues to evolve as criminals adopt increasingly sophisticated methods to target individuals, businesses, and organizations. Staying informed about emerging threats is one of the most effective ways to reduce risk and make safer decisions online.

Understanding scam techniques, recognizing warning signs, protecting personal information, and knowing how to respond after an incident can significantly reduce the impact of cybercrime.

Through education, responsible reporting, and continuous public awareness, every individual contributes to building a safer and more resilient digital community.
