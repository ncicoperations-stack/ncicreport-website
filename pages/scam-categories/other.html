WHEN TO USE THIS FORM

Other Report

Use this report if your incident does not fit any of the available reporting categories on the National Cyber Intelligence Center website.

Please provide a detailed description of what happened, when it occurred, who or what was involved, and why you believe the incident should be reported.

Include any available supporting information such as screenshots, emails, documents, website links, usernames, transaction records, photographs, or other relevant evidence that may assist with reviewing your submission.
