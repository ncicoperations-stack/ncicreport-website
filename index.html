REPORTING PROCESS

What Happens After You Submit a Report?

Every report follows a structured review process designed to support intelligence gathering and appropriate case assessment.

1

Submit Your Report

Complete the online reporting form with accurate incident information.

2

Reference Number Issued

Your submission receives a unique NCIC reference number for future tracking.

3

Intelligence Review

Information is reviewed to identify patterns, indicators, and investigative value.

4

Case Assessment

Reports are evaluated and prepared for the appropriate investigative workflow.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Is there a cost to submit a report?

No. Submitting a report through the NCIC reporting portal is free.

Can I track my report?

Yes. Use the reference number issued after submission to check your report status.

What information should I prepare?

Provide accurate details about the incident, dates, communication methods, and supporting information requested by the reporting form.
